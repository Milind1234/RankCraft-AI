💡 Key ML Interview Talking Points

1. Why LambdaMART over pointwise regression?
Pointwise models treat each item independently, ignoring the competitive context of a search results page. LambdaMART uses lambda gradients (combining pairwise classification loss with NDCG rank-discount weighting $\Delta\text{NDCG}$) to directly optimize the top of the search ranking.
2. Why Bi-Encoder for Stage 1/2 vs Cross-Encoder for Stage 3?
Bi-Encoders encode query and product independently, allowing offline pre-computation of all catalog embeddings and sub-5ms cosine dot-product search. Cross-Encoders are too computationally heavy to evaluate thousands of catalog items per request.
3. How is Position Bias handled?
In real search logs, items at rank #1 get more clicks simply due to visual placement. We apply empirical Bayes smoothing on historical impressions and use multi-level relevance grading (Impression = 0, Click = 1, Cart = 2, Purchase = 3) to reward true user commitment.
